# Sashimono Studio

A browser app for designing and planning small Japanese-style joinery pieces (sashimono): 3D model, cut list, joinery and build steps.

Open it here: https://dampierr8.github.io/sashimono-studio/

The whole app is the single file `index.html`. Updating that file updates the web page.

## Three modes

- **Design**: pick a piece type and starting point, or choose **Your own shape** to draw any straight-sided footprint (a pentagon, a rectangle with a corner cut off, an L-shape, a bay…). Make it as a box or planter, a cabinet or shelf unit (with open sides), or a table on legs. The studio mitres every wall at its own angle and offers three corner joints: mitre with bamboo pins, mitre with feather keys, and mitre with a hidden spline. Lengths and angles can be typed or the corners dragged; the Drawings tab adds a shape plan with every mitre angle, and a full-size template can be downloaded for printing.
- **Van furniture** (a piece type in Design): light pieces for van conversions in Japanese style, with a visible timber frame of square or rectangular posts and pegged rails holding thin plywood panels glued into grooves. An engawa bench with a slatted front, kaidan step boxes with lift-off treads and a round window, a todana overhead locker with kōshi sliding doors, and a mizuya kitchen unit with staggered shelves. Shows an estimated weight, plywood sheet counts and steps for fixing it in the van.
- **Practice**: a path of 16 small pieces from Beginner to Expert, each designed to teach one or two joints, with goals, a "made it" tick and progress.

### Joint exercises

Practice mode also has a **Joint exercises** list: 15 short bench exercises on offcuts (sawing, planing, pinned rebates, housings, angled mitres, half-laps, finger joints, mortise and tenon, draw-bored pegs, keyed mitres, wedged through tenons, dovetails, butterfly keys, half-blind dovetails and mitred bridles). Each one has a twist, beginner-friendly steps you can tick off, tips, common catches and a 3D close-up of the joint.

## Theory & history

A reference section: a searchable glossary of Japanese joinery terms, techniques and tools, a short history, and famous works (Tamamushi Shrine, Horyu-ji pagoda, Shoso-in, Ise, Kiyomizu-dera, Dojinsai, kaidan-dansu) shown as representative 3D models, each with an explodable close-up of a joint used in it.
